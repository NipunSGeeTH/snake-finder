# User guide

SnakeID works without a network. The first screen is Identify.

## Identify

Use **Take photo** or **Gallery**. The camera asks for permission the first time. Stand back. Include the whole snake and, if you can do it safely, the head. Do not catch the snake to improve the picture.

You can also tap a bundled sample. Those six photos are stored in the app so the model can be tried with no signal. They are not a promise about the next photo you take.

While the model runs, the screen shows a progress indicator. Then it shows one of these results:

- **A species**, with a venom badge, a percentage, and up to two other scores.
- **Low confidence** when the top score is under 55%. Open the dossier and compare the field marks. If the top names are cobras, remember that a rat snake raises its neck and is often killed by mistake.
- **No snake recognized**. The picture may be a plant, a blur, or an animal the model rejects. A bite can still have happened.
- **Species not recognized**. Do not treat that as harmless.
- **Identification unavailable**. The model did not run. Do not invent a name from the colors in the photo.

Every result tells you to go to hospital if someone was bitten. The percentage is not a diagnosis.

## Field guide

The guide lists 19 species, which is more than the camera can name. Search by English name, scientific name, local name, or habitat. Filter by venom status. Open a card for field marks, behavior, bite effects, first aid, and look-alikes.

The hump-nosed pit viper is in the guide because it causes many bites in Sri Lanka and southwest India. It is not a class in the camera model.

## First aid

The First aid tab is general public guidance for the time before a hospital takes over. It includes:

- 1990, the Suwa Seriya ambulance in Sri Lanka
- 011 269 1111, National Hospital of Sri Lanka accident service, also listed as poison information
- 119, Sri Lanka police
- 108 and 112 in India
- 911 in the United States and 112 in much of Europe

**Call 1990** opens the phone dialer. It does not place the call until you confirm.

The steps are: move away, keep the person still, splint the limb without a tight tourniquet, remove rings and watches, photograph the snake only if that does not delay transport, and go to a hospital. Cutting, sucking, ice, shock, alcohol, and aspirin are listed as things not to do. Antivenom is given in hospital.

## Model

The Model tab describes the single built-in network and lists its 14 classes in label order. It is there so you can see what the camera is allowed to say. There is no download button.

## History

Each completed identification is saved on the phone, newest first, up to 50. Times use the phone's local clock. **Clear** deletes the file. History does not upload anything. A row that names a guide species opens that dossier.
