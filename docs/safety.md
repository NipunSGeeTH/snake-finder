# Safety

SnakeID helps a person describe a snake and remember first aid. It does not diagnose a bite, choose antivenom, or replace a clinician.

## How to treat a result

- A high percentage can still be the wrong species. The bundled model gives some sample photos a score near 1.0, and it also gives a flower a deadly-viper score unless the non-snake guard fires.
- "Non-venomous" means the named species is not venomous. It does not mean this photograph was read correctly.
- "No snake" means this model did not recognize a snake. It does not mean nobody was bitten.
- "Low confidence" means the top classes are close or the score is under 55%. Read the look-alikes.
- Several dangerous snakes are absent from the model, including the hump-nosed pit viper (*Hypnale hypnale*). Absence from the label list is not evidence that a local snake is harmless.
- If someone was bitten, go to a hospital whether or not the app has a photo.

## First aid this app will and will not give

The emergency text follows the public steps used for snakebite while transport is arranged: leave the snake alone, keep the person still, splint the limb, remove constricting items, and reach a hospital that can watch breathing, clotting, and kidneys.

The app tells you not to cut the wound, suck it, apply a tight tourniquet, ice it, shock it, or give alcohol or aspirin.

It does not give drug doses. It does not tell you to inject antivenom. Species pages that used to name a product for the user to administer now say the hospital makes that decision.

Krait bites can have almost no pain. Waiting for dramatic symptoms is not a reason to stay home.

## Phone numbers

1990 is the nationwide Suwa Seriya ambulance number in Sri Lanka. 011 269 1111 is the widely published number for the National Hospital of Sri Lanka accident service and for poison information. 108 and 112 are the ambulance and national emergency numbers in India. Confirm local numbers if you are somewhere else. The app cannot know which hospital stocks which antivenom.

## Privacy

Photos are decoded on the phone. They are not uploaded. History stores the name, score, time, and a short note. It does not store the image. Uninstalling the app, or tapping Clear, removes that history.
