# How to photograph a snake

Stand back. Do not catch, corner, or move the animal to get a better picture.

## What to include

- The whole snake, from head to tail, if you can do it from a safe distance.
- A second photo of the head, from the side, if the first photo is only the body.
- The ground or the branch around it, so the photo is not a tight crop of scales.

## What makes a photo fail

A photo of a screen, a drawing, or a snake that fills only a corner of the frame is a weak photo. The app can miss the animal or name the wrong species. Take another picture rather than trusting a single unclear one.

Flash at night helps the pattern. It also startles the snake. Keep the light brief and stay back.
