# Look-alikes

Several harmless snakes are killed because they share a color or a posture with a dangerous one. The model makes the same mistake.

## Cobra and rat snake

A rat snake raises the front of its body. It has large eyes and dark bars on the lips, and it does not spread a true hood. A cobra spreads a hood and has a rounder eye.

## Krait and wolf snake

Both can show pale bands on a dark body. A krait has enlarged hexagonal scales along the spine. A wolf snake does not, and it is active around houses in the day more often than a krait.

## Viper and python

Russell's viper has chains of oval spots and a triangular head with a vertical pupil. A python's blotches are irregular, and the scales are smooth. The wide catalog sometimes swaps these.

If two of these are both plausible, keep both names and do not settle on one from the photo alone.
